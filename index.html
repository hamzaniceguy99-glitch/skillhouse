How we work

Adults only, and no distilling

This is for people 21 and over. Skillhouse sells no beer, ingredients or equipment and takes no commission, so nothing here is steering you toward a shopping list.

Two firm limits. Home brewing for personal use is legal within federal and state limits, and selling what you brew without a license is not — know your state's rules. And we never cover distilling: doing it at home without federal permits is a felony, and no amount of enthusiasm changes that.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Extract or all-grain?

Extract makes excellent beer and removes a whole set of variables. Move to all-grain when you want more control, not because someone implied it's more serious.

My beer tastes like green apple.

Usually acetaldehyde — often beer packaged too early or fermentation that finished cold. Send your log and we'll trace it.

How do I control temperature cheaply?

A plastic tub, water, a towel and frozen bottles hold a remarkably steady temperature. We'll set it up with you before you buy anything.

Can I sell my beer?

No, not without licensing. Home brewing is for personal use, and we won't help anyone work around that.

Do you cover distilling?

Never. Distilling at home without federal permits is illegal, and it's outside what we coach entirely.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
