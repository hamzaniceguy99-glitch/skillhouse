The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, brewed on your own equipment with your own logs.

Program 01 · Process before recipes

Your first batches

We brew a simple batch together, step by step. Sanitation is the first hour because it causes most beginner failures, then the boil, chilling, pitching, and taking gravity readings that tell you what's actually happening. Packaging gets its own session — overprimed bottles are dangerous.

  • 8 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • One batch a month
  • A basic kit

Program 02 · The biggest lever

Fermentation control

Yeast makes the beer; you just make the wort. We cover choosing strains, pitching enough healthy yeast, making starters, and controlling temperature with cheap methods — a tub of water and a towel beats an uncontrolled closet by a wide margin.

  • 8 weeks
  • Brewed a few batches
  • One batch a month
  • A thermometer

Program 03 · Your own beer

Recipe design

We move from kits to your own recipes: reading style guidelines, building a grain bill, balancing bitterness against malt, hop timing and dry hopping, and basic water adjustments. Then iterating a single recipe over several batches until it's yours.

  • All-grain or extract
  • One batch a month
  • Brewing software

Program 04 · Name it, then fix it

Off-flavors & faults

Green apple, band-aid, wet cardboard, butter — each points at something specific in the process. We taste and describe methodically, work backwards from flavor to cause, and tighten the step that produced it. Most brewers fix three habits and never taste those faults again.

  • Brewing regularly
  • One batch a month
