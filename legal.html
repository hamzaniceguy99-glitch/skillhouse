Terms of service

What we provide

Skillhouse provides home brewing coaching delivered remotely by video to adults aged 21 and over. Skillhouse sells, supplies and ships no alcohol, ingredients or equipment and takes no retailer commission. Home brewing for personal use is subject to federal and state limits and selling home-brewed beer without a license is illegal; distillation at home is illegal without federal permits and is never covered here. Brewers are responsible for their own sanitation, pressure equipment and safe practice. Programs are described on the Programs page and prices on the home page. No physical goods are sold or shipped.

Pricing and billing

Prices are in US dollars and range from $29 to $149 per month depending on the plan. Plans are billed monthly in advance on the same day each month through [PAYMENT PROVIDER]. Applicable sales tax, if any, is added at checkout. The 20-minute batch review is free and comes with no obligation. Participants must be 21 or over.

Cancellation

There is no contract or minimum term. Cancel any time and your access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for. You will not be billed again after you cancel.

Refunds

If you cancel within 14 days of your first payment and have used no more than one session, we refund that payment in full. After that, payments already made are non-refundable, but you keep access for the rest of the paid month.

Rescheduling and missed sessions

You may reschedule a one-to-one session at no cost up to 24 hours before the start time. Later than that, or if you do not show up, the session counts as delivered.

No guarantees

Coaching depends heavily on the work you put in. We do not promise any specific result about how any batch will taste. Services are provided on an "as is" basis, and our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the three months before the claim.

Program materials are for your personal use. Sharing your account, recording sessions without consent or redistributing materials may end your access without a refund.
